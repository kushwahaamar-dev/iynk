Freetail Hackers
Beginner Friendly Open Ended Social Good
HackTX is an annual hackathon at the University of Texas at Austin. A hackathon is a space for students of any major to come together and come up with creative solutions using technology. They are a place to learn, to meet others, and to innovate. We design our hackathons to be beginner-friendly, and we will have plenty of mentors and workshops to guide you. 
Check out https://hacktx.com/ for additional information!
Spirit of the Competition
Remember that hackathons are like marathons. Some people go to compete, but most people take part to better themselves and have fun. Regardless of why you're at a hackathon, ensure you're upholding the hacker spirit and Community Values by collaborating with other teams, helping beginners, and having fun.
REQUIREMENTS
All projects must be built during the hacking period from 11:30 am CST on October 24th, 2026 to 11:30 am CST on October 25th, 2026. The project should be submitted to this DevPost, and team member(s) must be present in person during judging to be considered! Additionally, a video demoing your project must be submitted to this DevPost by the submission deadline. Team size must be between 1-4 people.

JUDGES
Freetail Hackers
JUDGING CRITERIA
Technology
How technically impressive or difficult was the hack? Did you use clever techniques, multiple components, or a complex setup that made the judges go "wow"?
Design
How much thought was put into the user experience (UX) and interface design? For software, this covers clean CSS and graphics; for hardware, it focuses on human-computer interaction and ease of use.
Completion
Does the working hack actually function? Did you accomplish what you set out to build during the time limit?
Learning
Did you stretch your skills and try to learn something new? Shifting out of one's comfort zone (e.g., a VR developer building a mobile app) is actively rewarded.
