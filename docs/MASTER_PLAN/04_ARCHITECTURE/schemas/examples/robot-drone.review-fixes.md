## Domain rules (V7, in `tools/project-lib.mjs`)

Implemented because JSON Schema cannot express them: RB-E-TREE (one root, one parent per body, no cycle), RB-E-MASS, RB-E-THIN for `from-shape` colliders, RB-E-AXIS, RB-E-LIMITS, RB-E-DRIVE (the component exists; the `mechanics` block check needs the part descriptors, which are not in the document), RB-E-WHEEL (a `WheelSpec` exists, the axis is horizontal within 1 degree in the rest pose, computed through the frame chain), RB-E-SURFACE, sensor mount and component agree, ROS references resolve; DB-C-12 (`pid_hz` divides and does not exceed `step_hz`, gyro filter below Nyquist), the 90-degree thrust-axis rule of 5.3, unique ESC channels, motor mounts hold the named motor. Warnings: `prop_spin != spin` (legal, DB-C-11) and spin directions that do not cancel. Not implemented: RB-E-INERTIA (the document has no inertia for these bodies, derived from shape), RB-W-* warnings, DB-C-01..10 and DB-C-13..14 (they need part data).

## Assumptions in the examples that need a decision from the spec owners

1. **Joint frames.** PDM 5.8 does not say in which frame `Joint.origin` and `Joint.axis` are given. I used the URDF reading (ROBOTICS 4.1 already says "fixed axes of the parent frame (URDF)"): `origin` is the joint frame in the parent body's frame, `axis` is in the joint (child) frame. The wheel body frame is rotated -90 degrees about x so the cylinder axis (local z) lies along world y, and the axis is `[0, 0, 1]`. Positive rotation drives the robot toward +x (worked by hand: omega times r at the contact point). If PDM 5.8 picks another convention the two joints change.
2. **Quad-X motor order and spin.** Motor 1 rear right CCW, 2 front right CW, 3 rear left CW, 4 front left CCW (the usual Betaflight props-in layout; EXTERNAL, recalled, not checked against the Betaflight docs today). The validator only checks that the spins cancel. Positions assume a 225 mm wheelbase (motors at plus or minus 79.5 mm), x forward, y left, FLU.
3. **Joint records.** `domains.robotics.data.joints` has entries for the two wheel joints only; the fixed caster joint has none (ROBOTICS 4.3 types the record by joint id and does not say fixed joints need an entry). The validator does not require one.
4. **Wiring choices that are teaching points, not errors:** ENA and ENB jumpers removed (`ena_jumper: false`) so PWM on D5 and D6 sets speed; battery 7.4 V feeds `VS` and the Uno `VIN`; the L298N 5 V pin is left unconnected; the sketch caps the base PWM at 140 of 255 because the pack is above the 6 V motor rating.
5. **Fault ids** use the cause ids of SIM 13.4 (`motor-direction-reversed`, `sensor-too-high`, `battery-sag-reset`, `props-swapped`, `battery-sag-brownout`). The symptom ids (`robot-turns-wrong-way`, `line-not-detected`, `robot-resets-at-start`, `flips-on-takeoff`, `yaw-spin-on-takeoff`, `fc-resets-in-flight`) are mine: no symptom registry exists yet (D-010 fixes the record, SIM_ARCH 13.4 owns the registry).
