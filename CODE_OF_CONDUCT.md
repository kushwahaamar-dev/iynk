# Code of Conduct

iynk is a place to learn how electronics, robots and drones work. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests
and discussions, is expected to keep it friendly and useful.

## What we expect

- Be kind. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Welcome beginners. A basic question is a fine question.
- Give credit, and say where facts and text came from.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or unwelcome attention.
- Personal attacks, or sharing someone's private information.
- Sexual or discriminatory language or images.
- Spam, or pushing a product on people who did not ask.

## When something goes wrong

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use GitHub's report tools on the comment or profile, or ask a maintainer
in an issue without naming the person. If the problem involves a maintainer, report it to GitHub.

This applies in the repository and anywhere a person speaks for the project.
