@AGENTS.md

Before doing anything in this repo, run `bash scripts/handoff-status.sh`, then read `HANDOFF.md` and follow its
"How to resume" steps. It holds the live build state, who is driving, the next step and the rules (hackathon rule,
commit authorship, secrets, what needs the user's OK). `docs/handoff/CONTEXT.md` holds the users' requests, the
decisions so far and the conversation log. Two people take turns driving: take over and hand over the way HANDOFF.md
says, and commit and push after every step.
