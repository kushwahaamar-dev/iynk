# Fig. 07 — A4988 stepper driver (Robotics Bench, Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric bench figure on the pale green cutting mat with a flat blue cast shadow, mint
palette accent. In the middle the **green Pololu A4988 carrier** the learner builds (figures/modules/a4988.js:
15.24 x 20.32 mm, the A4988 in its 5 x 5 mm QFN-28, the current-limit trimpot whose metal rotor is the VREF node, two
white R068 sense resistors, the 4.7 µF ceramic on VMOT, the small 0603 parts, two 1 x 8 headers soldered long pins
up, a jumper cap across SLP and RST and the 100 µF electrolytic lying on the mat with its legs on VMOT and GND). On
the mat: an **Arduino Nano** in front of the control header, a **NEMA 17 stepper** (42.3 mm square, 38 mm body,
200 steps a turn, 1.68 A a phase) behind it with its four leads plugged onto 2B, 2A, 1A, 1B, a **2S LiPo** behind on
the right on VMOT and GND, and a yellow **pocket multimeter** in front on the right with its red probe on the pot's
rotor and its black probe on a GND pin, set to 2 V DC. Jumpers: D3 to STEP, D4 to DIR, D5-D7 to MS1-MS3, the Nano's
5V to VDD and GND to GND.

What it does:

- **Set up the mat ▾**: the Nano with its wires, then the motor, then the pack, then the meter, one step at a time,
  never live.
- **Upload sketch ▾**: Hold (coils on, no steps: the sketch for setting the limit), Full step (200 steps at 5 ms, one
  turn a second, then a second's pause), Half step (MS1 high, 400 steps at 5 ms), 1/16 step (MS1-MS3 high, 3200
  steps at 1.25 ms, a quarter turn a second, smooth). The motor's rotor turns by the sketch's own step count, so a
  full turn lands on a full turn.
- The model (Allegro A4988 datasheet, docs/facts/a4988.md): the pot starts at 0.30 V; while Hold runs you turn it and
  the meter climbs to 0.544 V, which is I_MAX = VREF / (8 x 0.068 Ω) = 1.0 A. VREF follows VDD. Each coil's current
  comes from the rotor's electrical angle (50 teeth), snapped to the mode's rest positions: 70.7 % of I_MAX in each
  coil at a full step, the sine table at 1/16. The heat is I_MAX² through 0.64 Ω of switches (rising as it warms)
  into the bare carrier at 60 °C/W (assumed), with thermal shutdown at 165 °C until it has cooled by 15 °C. Under 8 V
  on VMOT the outputs drop out and steps are lost. The motor's coils light up in the see-through view.
- It reports `state.levels` (STEP, DIR, MS1-MS3) and `meas.U1` (vref, pot, set, imax, limit_mA, coil_mA, tj, trip,
  dead, ia, ib), `meas.MOT` (state, deg, ia, ib, rpm), `meas.BATT` (state, v, i_mA), `meas.MM` (what the meter shows).
- **Break it ▾**: the LiPo backwards (no protection: the driver burns), the 100 µF backwards (it vents), the SLP-RST
  cap off (RST floats: the driver sits in reset), a tired pack at 7.4 V (under 8 V: the outputs drop out and steps
  are lost), the coil pairs mixed (1A and 2A swapped: it buzzes in place), the pot turned up to the motor's 1.68 A
  (the chip passes 165 °C and the thermal shutdown cycles), the motor unplugged while it runs (the coil's L·di/dt
  spike kills the A4988).

State the host reads: `status`, `power`, `mode`, `matStep`, `rails.VMOT`, `rails.VDD`, `levels.*`, `meas.U1.*`,
`meas.MOT.*`, `meas.BATT.*`, `meas.MM.*`, `faults`, `missing`. The lesson is `src/content/robotics/a4988.ts`; the
facts are `docs/facts/a4988.md`.

Look: Colorfigures palette, warm paper, heavy ink silhouette, finer inner lines, hand-drawn wobble, paper grain. Works
on a phone and inside the SimLab host (embedded, bare).
