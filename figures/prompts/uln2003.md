# ULN2003 stepper driver board (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of the **ULN2003 driver board that comes with the 28BYJ-48 stepper**,
raised on four short standoffs over a pale green cutting mat with a flat blue cast shadow, mint palette
(`figures/modules/uln2003.js`, facts in `docs/facts/la-components.md` §15.2; the layout is the common one read from
product photos, so the size is marked unverified). The board is green, 35 x 32 mm, 1.6 mm thick, with four 3 mm
holes in the corners and white silkscreen, and on it:

- the **ULN2003APG** Darlington array in a DIP-16 package in the middle, notch to the left, silk "ULN2003" above;
- the white 5-pin **JST-XH** motor socket (2.5 mm pitch, latch ramp on the back wall, "1" by pin 1) at the top edge,
  with the motor's plug in it and its five wires, blue, pink, yellow, orange and red, arching back over the board
  edge and off the back of the mat;
- the **IN1-IN4** 1x4 header at the bottom left, the power pins **− +** marked "5-12V" beside it, and the two power
  jumper pins with a black cap on them, silk "ON";
- four red SMD LEDs **A-D** down the right side, each with its 1 kΩ resistor printed "102", and the copper traces
  from each input to the chip, from the chip to the socket and from + through the jumper.

What it does:

- **Power ▾**: a 5 V supply on − + (the board takes 5-12 V), or Unplug. Power reaches the chip only through the
  jumper cap.
- **Upload sketch ▾** switches instantly between Uno sketches as the board's inputs see them; LED A-D light with
  IN1-IN4, and once a second the host's serial monitor gets the step, the coils on and the current, each coil
  taking (V − 1.0 V) / 50 Ω, 80 mA at 5 V:
  - **Full step, slowed down** (the default): IN1+IN2, IN2+IN3, IN3+IN4, IN4+IN1 at two steps a second
    ("step 0 · IN1+IN2 · 2 coils · 160 mA").
  - **Full step at 500 steps/s**: every LED half lit, "500 steps/s ≈ 14.7 RPM · 2 coils on at a time · 160 mA".
  - **Half step, slowed down**: eight steps, one coil and two coils in turn, four steps a second.
  - **Wave drive, slowed down**: one coil at a time, 80 mA.
  - **Wrong pin order 8, 9, 10, 11**: IN1+IN3, IN2+IN3, IN2+IN4, IN1+IN4, both halves of one coil on together every
    other step (on a motor it only jitters).
  - **Too fast: 1000 steps/s**: the LEDs still cycle, "1000 steps/s is past the pull-out rate: the motor hums and
    stalls, the LEDs still cycle".
  - **disableOutputs()**: every LED off, "coils off · 0 mA".
- The LEDs hang from + through their 1 kΩ resistors, so they dim on a low supply.
- **See-through** shows the chip's die with its bond wires and current dots along the IN1-IN4 traces and the +
  supply path. Labels: SEVEN DARLINGTON PAIRS, COILS: BLUE PINK YELLOW ORANGE, POWER ON JUMPER.
- **Strip to bare PCB, Pick & place SMD, Place a part ▾, Solder joints, Remove a part ▾**: the engine's build order,
  the chip and each LED placeable either way round. Without the jumper cap or the chip nothing is powered.
- **Break it ▾**: reverse polarity on − + (the ULN2003 burns), too high a voltage (24 V: the chip is damaged) or too
  low (the LEDs dim), a brown-out, the chip in backwards (hot), an LED in backwards (dark), cold joints on the input
  header, the motor socket, the chip and the LEDs.
- **View ▾**: the board, the schematic and power-tree sheets, the exploded view.

Look: warm paper, heavy ink outline with finer inner lines, flat light/mid/dark bands, the five Colorfigures
palettes.
