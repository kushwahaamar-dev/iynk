# SSD1306 0.96" OLED module, 128 x 64, I²C (Colorfigures prompt)

Two figures share this prompt: the module on its own (`figures/modules/ssd1306.js`) and the breadboard part that
lesson circuits use (`figures/parts/ssd1306.js`, in the Uno R3's "Smart plant" circuit as OLED1). Facts in
`docs/facts/la-components.md` §17.

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of the common **0.96 inch I²C OLED module**: a blue 27.3 x 27.8 mm
board, 1.2 mm thick, with four 2 mm holes in the corners and the 4-pin 2.54 mm header along the top edge, silk pin
names over it (GND VCC SCL SDA), with:

- the dark glass panel (26.7 x 19.3 x 1.45 mm) taped to the front, its 21.7 x 10.9 mm active area of 128 x 64
  pixels (0.17 mm each), the long SSD1306 chip-on-glass die on the ledge below it, and an orange flex tail folding
  round the bottom edge to the back;
- on the back, hidden by the board until see-through: the 662K 3.3 V regulator and its input capacitor, the two
  charge-pump capacitors, the VCOMH capacitor, the SCL and SDA pull-ups to VCC and the address resistor in the 0x78
  position (7-bit 0x3C).

On the module page it stands on four gold standoffs over a pale green mat with a flat blue cast shadow, the header
pins pointing down (cobalt palette). As a breadboard part it lies flat on its header in four holes (a5-a8 on the
part page), the board and glass reaching out from the header row; its glass stays dark, and in a lesson circuit it
glows faintly blue, with a blue edge, while the sketch runs and the module has power. **Labels** reads "P1 · 0x3C".

What it does (module page):

- **Power ▾**: 5 V or 3.3 V from the board, or Unplug. The 662K gives the panel 3.3 V (about 3.05 V from a 3.3 V
  supply); below 3 V on that rail the pixels dim.
- **Upload sketch ▾** switches the sketch instantly. The screen is a real 128 x 64 frame buffer drawn with a 5 x 7
  font in 6-pixel cells, lit pixels in white:
  - **Moisture screen** (the default): the percentage in big digits, a bar across the screen that fills to it, and
    the mood below: "Water me!" under 30 %, "Too wet!" over 85 %, else "Happy :)". The value drifts between 11 and
    93 %, and the serial monitor gets "Moisture: 52%" once a second.
  - **Hello, world**: a frame round the screen, "Hello, world!", "iynk" in double size and "128 x 64 at 0x3C";
    the serial monitor gets "display.begin() ok".
  - **Pixel test**: a checkerboard of 8-pixel squares on the top 16 rows that flips twice a second, diagonals and a
    16-pixel grid below.
  - **I²C scanner**: the screen stays dark; the serial monitor prints "Scanning..." and "I2C device found at
    address 0x3C" in turn.
  - **Wrong address 0x3D**: the screen stays dark, and the monitor says "begin(0x3D) returned true, but nothing
    answers at 0x3D: the screen stays dark".
  - **Display off**: dark.
- The host can send its own frame (the 1024-byte GDDRAM in SSD1306 page order) with the address, on/off, invert,
  contrast and the panel colour: white, blue, or yellow-blue (the top 16 rows yellow). A frame sent to any address
  but 0x3C leaves the screen blank.
- **See-through** shows the back parts through the board, the ITO rows and metal columns crossing over the active
  area, the die lit on the ledge, and current dots along VCC, 3V3, SCL and SDA. Label: SSD1306 DIE ON THE GLASS.
- **Strip to bare PCB, Pick & place SMD, Place a part ▾, Solder joints, Remove a part ▾**: the engine's build order;
  without the glass or either charge-pump capacitor the panel never lights.
- **Break it ▾**: reverse polarity on either supply (the regulator burns), too high a voltage (10 V: the regulator is
  damaged) or too low (the panel dims to nearly dark), a brown-out, cold joints on the header, the glass and the
  charge-pump capacitors.
- The `?variant=vcc-gnd` page prints the other makers' pin order, VCC GND SCL SDA.
- **View ▾**: the board, the schematic and power-tree sheets, the exploded view.

Look: warm paper, heavy ink outline with finer inner lines, flat light/mid/dark bands, the five Colorfigures
palettes.
