# Fig. P11 — TIP120 Darlington transistor (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of a **TIP120 NPN Darlington transistor** standing in a solderless
breadboard, on the pale green cutting mat with a flat blue cast shadow. TO-220 package, real size: black epoxy body
10.0 x 9.0 x 4.5 mm with "TIP120" and "B C E" printed on the front, the bare copper tab (the collector) across the
whole back rising to 15.5 mm with its 3.7 mm mounting hole, and three 0.8 mm tinned leads at 2.54 mm, wide at the
shoulder, narrow where they go into the holes. With the printed face toward you the leads are base, collector,
emitter, left to right; the body sits about 4.5 mm above the breadboard on the lead shoulders.

What it does:

- In a lesson circuit (lesson 10 motor, lesson 11 fan, Robotics Fig 03) it is a low-side switch: a small base
  current (about 3.7 mA through 1 kΩ from a 5 V pin) lets up to amps flow collector to emitter. hFE ≥ 1000,
  VBE 1.2-1.4 V and VCE(sat) 0.7-1.0 V at 0.1-0.3 A, so a 5 V motor sees about 4 V. A dot on the face glows with
  the collector current and the label shows it in mA.
- **Put in backwards ▾**: turned round in the same holes, emitter and base change places: the motor barely runs.
- **Show ▾** (part page): off, switching 200 mA, overheating (2 A with no heatsink, 1.95 W of its 2 W free-air
  rating: the body tints red with heat shimmer), burnt (junction past 150 °C: smoke, fails short).
- **See-through**: the epoxy ghosts to show the single silicon die soldered to the tab, with the driver transistor
  Q1, the output transistor Q2, the R1 8 kΩ / R2 120 Ω strip, and aluminium bond wires to the base and emitter
  leads; the middle lead joins the tab. A spark of current runs down the emitter wire while it conducts. Labels:
  Q1 driver transistor, Q2 output transistor, Tab = collector, Base bond wire.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, mustard accents, flat light / mid / dark bands, heavy silhouette, finer inner
lines. Facts: docs/facts/la-components.md §11 (Fairchild TIP120 sheet).
