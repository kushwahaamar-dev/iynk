# MPU-6050 accelerometer and gyroscope, GY-521 breakout (Colorfigures prompt)

Two figures share this prompt: the module on its own (`figures/modules/mpu6050.js`) and the breadboard part that
lesson circuits use (`figures/parts/mpu6050.js`). Facts in `docs/facts/mpu6050.md`.

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of a **GY-521** board: blue, 15.6 x 20.3 mm, two Ø3 mm holes on the
right, an 8-pin header on the left edge (VCC GND SCL SDA XDA XCL AD0 INT, VCC at the top), with:

- the 4 x 4 mm MPU-6050 QFN in the middle, a 662K SOT-23 3.3 V regulator by VCC, a 10 µF tantalum on the right,
  0603 capacitors and "472" 4.7 kΩ resistors (SDA and SCL pull-ups to 3.3 V, the AD0 pull-down), a "102" 1 kΩ
  resistor and the red power LED at the bottom, silkscreen pin names, "ITG/MPU" and the X and Y axis arrows.
- On the module page it stands on its header pins above a pale green mat; as a breadboard part the header goes in
  one row and the board lies flat over the rows beside it, component side up, its LED lit when the circuit gives
  VCC a live rail and GND a ground.

What it does (module page):

- **Programs** are what a host sketch prints, one line a second: "Rocking about X" (ay and az follow a ±30° roll,
  the gyro reads the rate plus a 2.4 °/s bias), "Lying flat", "Never woken up" (all zeros and 36.5 °C: the chip
  powers up asleep) and "I2C scan" (0x68, or 0x69 without the AD0 pull-down).
- While it runs awake, an orange arrow above the chip shows gravity as the accelerometer sees it.
- **See-through**: the CMOS die with the MEMS die bonded on it, and current on VCC, 3.3 V, SDA and SCL.
- **Power**: 5 V or 3.3 V on VCC (the 662K drops it to about 3.05 V, still enough).
- **Break it**: reverse polarity or too high a voltage (the regulator burns), the CPOUT capacitor left out (the
  gyro reads noise), the AD0 pull-down left out (address 0x69), a cold joint on SDA (some reads fail), 3.3 V
  shorted to GND.

Look: warm paper, heavy ink outline with finer inner lines, flat bands, the five palettes.
