# Fig. 06 — L298N motor driver (Robotics Bench, Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric bench figure on the pale green cutting mat with a flat blue cast shadow, tomato
palette accent. In the middle the **red L298N module** the learner builds (figures/modules/l298n.js: 43 mm square,
the L298N in its Multiwatt15 package bolted to a black finned heatsink along the back edge, the 78M05, eight flyback
diodes, two 220 µF capacitors, three blue screw terminals, the IN1-IN4 header and the ENA, ENB and 5V-EN jumper
caps). On the mat: an **Arduino Nano** in front of the control header, a **2S LiPo** on the left, two **TT gear
motors** behind the heatsink with their wheels at the back. Jumpers: D8, D7, D5 and D4 to IN1-IN4, the Nano's GND to
the power terminal's GND, motor A on OUT1/OUT2, motor B on OUT3/OUT4, the pack on +12V and GND. The ENA and ENB caps
stay on, as the module ships.

What it does:

- **Set up the mat ▾**: the Nano with its wires, then the motors, then the pack, one step at a time, never live.
- **Upload sketch ▾**: Forward, Reverse, Spin on the spot, Brake, Half speed. Half speed writes 128 to ENA and ENB
  and changes nothing: the caps hold both enables at 5 V.
- The model (ST L298 datasheet): each bridge from IN and the caps, the two conducting bipolar switches dropping
  1.4 + 1.15 x I volts, the TT motor model on what is left: about 5.8 V and 210 rpm per motor from 7.4 V, 1.6 V lost
  in the bridge. Heat is the drop times the current plus the chip's quiescent draw, into a 15 °C/W fin; the junction
  follows it and the overtemperature cut-out trips at 130 °C until it has cooled by 20 °C. The stats panel shows the
  battery, both motors, the volts lost and the heatsink temperature; heat shimmer rises off the fins.
- It reports `state.levels` (IN1-IN4, ENA, ENB) and `meas.U1` (drop, heat, heatsink and junction temperature, trip),
  `meas.MOTA`, `meas.MOTB`, `meas.BATT`.
- **Break it ▾**: the LiPo backwards (the flyback diodes short it), C1 backwards (it vents), 6 V (the 78M05 has no
  headroom: the logic browns out), 36 V (past the regulator's and the capacitors' 35 V), a cold joint on motor A's
  terminal (it stutters), the 5V-EN cap off (no logic supply), no common ground (the inputs float and the motors
  twitch), both wheels held (about 6 W in the chip: the heatsink passes 100 °C and the cut-out cycles).

State the host reads: `status`, `power`, `mode`, `matStep`, `rails.VS`, `rails.5V`, `levels.*`, `meas.U1.*`,
`meas.MOTA.*`, `faults`, `missing`. The lesson is `src/content/robotics/l298n.ts`; the facts are
`docs/facts/l298n.md`.

Look: Colorfigures palette, warm paper, heavy ink silhouette, finer inner lines, hand-drawn wobble, paper grain. Works
on a phone and inside the iynk host (embedded, bare).
