# Fig. P13 — 1N4007 rectifier diode (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of two **1N4001-1N4007 rectifier diodes** (a 1N4007 and a 1N4001)
lying in a mini breadboard on the pale green cutting mat, with a flat blue cast shadow. DO-41 axial package, real
size: black epoxy body 4.8 mm long and 2.6 mm across with a silver band at the cathode end and the type printed
along it ("4007"), 0.8 mm tinned leads bent down into holes a few columns apart.

What it does:

- Forward drop from the SPICE fit (IS 7.03 nA, N 1.81, RS 34 mΩ): 0.56 V at 1 mA, 0.81 V at 0.2 A, 0.91 V at 1 A;
  1 A average, 30 A surge; VRRM from 50 V (1N4001) to 1000 V (1N4007): **Type ▾** picks the family member.
- Its two lesson jobs: the **flyback diode** across a motor or fan (band to the + supply, anode to the transistor's
  collector: it clamps the switch-off spike) and **reverse-polarity protection** in series with a supply.
- **Show ▾** (part page): off, conducting 200 mA (the label shows 0.81 V), blocking, clamping a motor spike, burnt.
- **See-through**: the epoxy ghosts to show the two tinned lead slugs and the glass-passivated silicon PN chip
  between them, glowing while current flows. Labels: Silicon PN chip, Cathode band.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, flat banded shading, a fine silver band. Facts: docs/facts/la-components.md §12
(Diodes Inc. 1N4001-1N4007 sheet).
