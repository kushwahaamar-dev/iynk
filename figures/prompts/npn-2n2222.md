# Fig. P12 — 2N2222 small-signal NPN transistor (Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric figure of two **2N2222A-family NPN transistors in TO-92 packages** side by
side in a mini breadboard on the pale green cutting mat, with flat blue cast shadows: a **PN2222A** (pins E B C
with the flat face toward you) and an **onsemi P2N2222A** (pins C B E), each printed with its name and pin order
on the flat face so the difference is the lesson. Real size: D-shaped black body 4.8 mm wide, 4.8 mm tall, 3.8 mm
deep, three 0.45 mm leads 1.27 mm apart under the body, bent out to the 2.54 mm breadboard pitch; the body sits
about 3 mm up.

What it does:

- In a circuit it is a small switch: saturate it with a base current of about IC / 10. Ratings: VCEO 40 V,
  IC 600 mA, 625 mW free air (200 °C/W), hFE 100-300 at 150 mA, VCE(sat) ≤ 0.3 V at 150 mA and ≤ 1.0 V at 500 mA.
- **Pinout ▾** (EBC / CBE) and **Put in backwards ▾**: the wrong guess swaps collector and emitter (it barely
  switches) or puts the base on the load side.
- **Show ▾** (part page): off, switching 100 mA, half on and hot (150 mA at 4 V = 0.6 W, the body tints red),
  burnt (smoke).
- **See-through**: the epoxy ghosts to show the lead frame, the silicon die sitting on the collector lead (the die's
  back is the collector) and two gold bond wires to base and emitter; the die glows while it conducts. Labels:
  Silicon die on the collector lead, Gold bond wires.

Look: Colorfigures ink and paper, mustard accents, flat banded shading. Facts: PN2222A (Fairchild-origin) and
P2N2222A (onsemi) datasheets.
