# Fig. 12 — LM2596 buck converter (Robotics Bench, Colorfigures prompt)

Make a colourful, hand-inked isometric bench figure on the pale green cutting mat with a flat blue cast shadow, cobalt
palette accent. In the middle the **blue LM2596 buck module with its voltmeter** the learner builds
(figures/modules/lm2596.js, the single-button 2596-SDC layout measured from a top-down vendor photo: 66 x 34 mm, four
M3 corner holes; the LM2596S-ADJ in its TO-263-5 with the tab toward the top edge and five gull-wing leads down
toward the SS34 catch diode; the black 33 µH shielded inductor printed `330` beside it; two black 220 µF 50 V
electrolytics, C1 by the input and C2 by the output; the blue 25-turn 3296W trimmer printed `W103` along the top edge
with its brass screw at the left end, the slot turning with the setting; a 3-digit red 7-segment display along the
bottom; the meter circuit's small parts and regulator to its left; the IN and OUT LEDs and the select button on the
right; blue 5 mm screw terminals on the left edge, IN+ above IN-, and on the right, OUT+ above OUT-). On the mat: a
**2S LiPo** on the left wired to IN+ and IN-, a yellow **pocket multimeter** in front with its probes on the output
screws, set to 20 V DC, and a **TT gear motor** with its wheel on the right, wired to OUT+ and OUT-.

What it does:

- **Set up the mat ▾**: the pack, then the meter, then the motor, one step at a time, never live. The motor goes on
  only after the output is set, with the power off: set the voltage with nothing on it, then connect the load.
- **Turn the trimmer ▾** (the modes are a screwdriver, not sketches): as delivered (the top of 25 turns, asking for
  38.5 V), 5 V (2.53 turns), 3.3 V (1.39 turns), 12 V (7.22 turns). The screw turns at five turns a second toward the
  setting, ticking each whole turn; a ding and a toast when the meter reads the voltage. Bench notes print the sum
  for the setting every five seconds.
- **Press SW1**: the display swaps between IN and OUT, and the IN or OUT LED lights. It boots showing IN.
- The model (TI LM2596 datasheet SNVS124G, docs/facts/lm2596.md): Vout = 1.23 x (1 + R2 / 330) with the trimmer at
  400 Ω a turn. Asked for more than the input can give, the switch stays on (100 % duty) and the output is the input
  less 0.745 V + 0.255 V/A. The switch's saturation (0.65 + 0.17 V/A), the diode's forward drop (0.35 + 0.05 V/A), the
  inductor's 0.08 Ω, switching (1.2 % of Vin x I, assumed) and 5 mA quiescent set the losses; the meter draws 15 mA
  from the input. About 74 % at 5 V into the motor. A 4.2 A average current limit; heat into the LM2596 at 50 °C/W
  and the SS34 at 80 °C/W (assumed), with a 150 °C thermal shutdown (assumed: the datasheet gives none). The display
  reads 0.03 V high (inside the vendors' ±0.1 V) and goes dark under 4 V. The motor is the TT model (4 Ω, about
  0.15 A free at 5 V, 1.25 A held). Shimmer over the LM2596 when it runs hot; the switch, the inductor and the
  feedback path light up in the see-through view.
- It reports `meas.U1` (vout, vset, duty, turns, set, sat, limited, eff, iout_mA, iin_mA, loss, tj, trip),
  `meas.DS1` (text, shows), `meas.D1` (dead, tc), `meas.MOT` (state, v, i_mA, rpm), `meas.BATT` (state, v, i_mA) and
  `meas.MM` (what the meter shows).
- **Break it ▾**: the LiPo backwards (nothing blocks it: the LM2596 burns), C1 backwards (it vents), a cold joint on
  the trimmer's wiper (FB sees 0 V: the output jumps to the input with a pop), the pack run flat to 5.5 V (no
  under-voltage lock-out: the output follows the pack down), the wheel held (1.25 A, far under the limit: the rail
  holds, the pack current climbs, the chip warms), OUT+ shorted to OUT- (4.2 A limit; the SS34 carries it, sizzles
  and fails after about six seconds; the display on IN still reads the input), and 44 V on the input (past the
  SS34's 40 V: it burns).

State the host reads: `status`, `power`, `mode`, `matStep`, `rails.VIN`, `leds.LED_IN`, `leds.LED_OUT`,
`controls.SW1`, `meas.U1.*`, `meas.DS1.*`, `meas.D1.*`, `meas.MOT.*`, `meas.BATT.*`, `meas.MM.*`, `faults`,
`missing`. The lesson is `src/content/robotics/lm2596.ts`; the facts are `docs/facts/lm2596.md`.

Look: Colorfigures palette, warm paper, heavy ink silhouette, finer inner lines, hand-drawn wobble, paper grain. Works
on a phone and inside the SimLab host (embedded, bare).
